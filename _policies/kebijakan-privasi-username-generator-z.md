# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 15, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **U-GenZ / Username Generator Z** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

The App is developed under the package name:

`com.belladyna.hurufgaul`

## 1. Information We Collect

U-GenZ is designed to work primarily as a local utility for generating usernames.

We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone access, or camera access in order to use the core username-generation features.

The App may store certain information locally on your device, such as:

- Favorite usernames
- Recently generated usernames
- Selected themes or vibes
- App preferences and settings

This locally stored information is used to provide App functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to us unless a third-party service described below processes related technical data.
